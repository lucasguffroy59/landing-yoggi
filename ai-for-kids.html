FAQ

Common questions about AI for kids

What is the best AI for kids?
The best AI for kids is one built specifically for children, not an adult tool with a filter applied afterward. Look for age-adapted responses, voice input for non-readers, real-time safety alerts to parents, and a parent dashboard, not just content filtering. Yoggi is built around these four principles for ages 3 to 15.
Is AI safe for kids to use?
It depends entirely on which AI. General-purpose tools like ChatGPT or Gemini were designed for adults: they add teen protections, but nothing built for young children. An AI purpose-built for children, like Yoggi, refuses inappropriate content by design, adapts its language to the child's age, and gives parents real-time alerts and full visibility into conversations.
What age can a child start using AI?
With the right tool, from age 3. Voice input lets children who can't yet read or type have a conversation by speaking, with responses using the simplest possible vocabulary. Yoggi supports children from age 3 through 15, adjusting automatically as they grow.
What is an AI chatbot for kids?
An AI chatbot for kids is a conversational AI designed specifically for children rather than adults: with age-adapted vocabulary, voice support for non-readers, refusal of inappropriate content by design, and visibility for parents into what their child is discussing. Yoggi is one example, built for ages 3 to 15.
Can a young child chat with an AI chatbot by voice?
Yes, with a chatbot designed for it. Yoggi supports full voice input and output, so children as young as 3, who can't yet read or type, can have a complete conversation by speaking and listening.
Does an AI chatbot for kids tell parents what was discussed?
The best ones do. Yoggi gives parents a full, PIN-protected chat history, a daily AI-generated insight, an optional weekly email recap, and an immediate email alert if a conversation ever needs attention.
