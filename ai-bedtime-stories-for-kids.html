FAQ

Common questions about Yoggi's bedtime stories

Can AI tell my child a bedtime story?
Yes. Yoggi can tell a full bedtime story out loud, in the same warm voice your child already knows from the app. Your child either describes an idea in a few words, by text or voice, or asks Yoggi to invent something as a complete surprise. Either way, the story comes out tailored to their age.
Is an AI bedtime story safe and age-appropriate?
Yes. Before any story is generated, a short preparation step checks the idea and gently steers away from anything not suited to your child's age, without ever just refusing. The story itself always uses simple, everyday vocabulary and ends on a calm, positive note, never on unresolved suspense right before bed.
Can my child listen with the screen off?
Yes. Once a story is ready, it keeps playing if your child locks the phone or turns off the screen, with play, pause, and skip controls available directly from the lock screen. It's built for listening in bed, not for staring at a screen.
Is the bedtime story feature free to try?
Yes. Every child gets one full bedtime story for life on the free plan, up to 5 minutes long. Premium accounts get a new story every day, up to 15 minutes long.
